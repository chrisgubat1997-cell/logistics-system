02 / CHALLENGE & OPPORTUNITY

Why Process Improvement Matters

In daily operations, information and transactions may involve different records, tools, monitoring activities, and coordination between people or departments. These can create opportunities to improve consistency, visibility, and ease of tracking. Rather than accepting repetitive manual work as part of the routine, we saw an opportunity to organize processes and make information easier to manage.

Brief explanation

Hindi ibig sabihin na mali ang existing process. Ang point natin ay hanapin kung saan puwedeng bawasan ang unnecessary steps, duplicate encoding, manual monitoring, o hirap sa pagkuha ng information.

06 / MEASUREMENT

How We Will Evaluate Improvement

Processing TimeCompare average task duration before and after.
Manual StepsIdentify steps simplified or automated.
AccuracyMonitor errors, corrections, and rework.
VisibilityAssess tracking and report preparation.

We understand that the presence of a system does not automatically mean productivity has improved. To evaluate its impact, we need to compare actual process performance using indicators such as processing time, manual steps, record accuracy, and reporting efficiency. These measurements will help us determine where improvements are achieved and where further work is needed.

Brief explanation

Ang mga ito ay measurement criteria pa lang. Maglalagay tayo ng actual before-and-after results kapag nakakuha na tayo ng validated data, para hindi tayo mag-claim ng improvement na hindi pa nasusukat.
